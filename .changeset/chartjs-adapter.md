Chart.js 4 adapter on the `highcharts-editable-excel/chartjs` subpath: `exportChartJsToXlsx`, `analyzeChartJsCompatibility`, `downloadChartJsAsXlsx`, `extractChartJsModel` for live charts or plain configs (line, bar, stacked, pie, doughnut, scatter, bubble, mixed, time scales, secondary axes). `chart.js` is an optional peer dependency.
